Privacy Policy for Quiz Hint Pro

Effective date: September 1, 2026

Quiz Hint Pro is a quiz app with optional consumable credits that can be used to reveal hints.

Local App Data

The app stores your credit balance and limited purchase-processing information locally on your device so that credits can be used correctly. No developer account or login is required.

Google Play Billing

Optional credit purchases are processed through Google Play Billing. Google may process purchase and payment information under Google's own privacy practices. The developer does not receive your full payment-card or banking information.

Quiz and Hint Use

Quiz answers, hints and credit balance are used to provide the app's features. The current version does not send quiz answers or credit balance to a developer-controlled server.

Advertising and Analytics

The current version does not use advertising SDKs or third-party analytics.

Data Sharing

The developer does not sell personal data. Purchase processing is handled by Google Play when you choose to buy credits.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact information shown on the Quiz Hint Pro Google Play listing.
